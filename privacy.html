✒️ Privacy Policy — Quill & Ledger

Effective date: November 16, 2026

The short version

Quill & Ledger collects nothing. It runs entirely in your browser, with no account, no server, and no network calls. Your work stays on your own device.

What the app does

Quill & Ledger is a freelance writer’s desk that works completely offline in your web browser. There is no account to create and no sign-in. The app makes no network requests: it does not send anything to a server, because there is no server.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, or receive any personal data, usage data, or analytics. The app sets no cookies and uses no trackers, ads, or third-party services.

Where your work lives

Everything you write is stored locally in your browser’s local storage, on your own device, and nowhere else. If you clear your browser’s stored data, your work in the app is deleted with it, so use the app’s backup and export features (Word, PDF, and other formats) to keep copies. Exported files are created on your device; they are never uploaded anywhere.

Sharing and selling data

We do not share or sell any data, because we have no data to share or sell.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and change the effective date above.
